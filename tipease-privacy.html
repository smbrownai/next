Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tipease ("the App") handles user information.

01

Information Collection

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal information. The only data entered into the App consists of user-provided inputs (e.g., check amount, tip calculations), which are used solely to perform on-device calculations.

02

Data Storage

All user inputs remain on the user's device and are not stored persistently by the App. The App does not maintain databases, user accounts, or any form of local or remote data retention.

03

Data Transmission

The App does not transmit any data off the device. No user inputs, usage data, or device information are sent to external servers, third parties, or analytics services.

04

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with or rely on third-party services, APIs, or SDKs that collect or process user data.

05

Security

Because the App does not collect or transmit data, there is no risk of personal data exposure through the App's operation. All calculations occur locally on the device.

06

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from users of any age, including children under 13.

07

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.

By using the App, you acknowledge that no personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted.
